## Brand & Style

This design system expresses modern kinetic vitality balanced by rigorous automotive engineering. It serves corporate logistics directors, school transport coordinators, and fleet managers seeking dependable passenger mobility alongside heavy-duty vehicle maintenance. The UI must project institutional authority without bureaucratic coldness, radiating human warmth, proactive safety, and surgical mechanical precision.

The aesthetic fuses contemporary rounded minimalism with structural bento-grid modularity. Tactile cards with generous internal negative space sit against luminous warm-white foundations. Dual visual tracks differentiate services instantaneously: amber-tinted treatments convey vitality, passenger comfort, and timeliness for transit operations; cobalt-tinted treatments signal diagnostics, heavy tooling, and technical warranty standards for the repair workshop.

## Colors

The chromatic architecture uses deep slate navy as the grounding anchor of authority and trust, paired with luminous amber and precision cobalt to create dynamic role-based hierarchy:

- **Primary Slate Navy (`#0F172A`, `#1E293B`):** Anchors global navigation, primary action buttons, structural headings, and footer frameworks. Imparts structural permanence.
- **Transportation Amber (`#F59E0B`, `#D97706`):** Dedicated to transit logistics, school bus service tags, active departure badges, primary CTA highlights, and dynamic fleet telemetry.
- **Technical Cobalt (`#2563EB`, `#1D4ED8`):** Allocated to maintenance booking flows, spare part logs, diagnostic readouts, inspection certifications, and technical badges.
- **Direct Connect Emerald (`#10B981`, `#059669`):** Reserved strictly for WhatsApp contact channels, verified mechanical safety badges, road assistance alerts, and operational confirmations.
- **Surfaces & Neutrals (`#FAFAFC`, `#FFFFFF`, `#F1F5F9`, `#64748B`):** Warm chalk backgrounds maintain visual clarity, with pure white cards isolating content modules and subtle slate tints handling secondary text and inactive states.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the friendly, organic geometry of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for titles, numerical displays, and labels with the neutral, engineered legibility of **Inter** for descriptions and tabular data. Both typefaces feature extensive Latin-Extended support, rendering Turkish characters (`ğ`, `ü`, `ş`, `ı`, `ö`, `ç`, `İ`) with balanced diacritics.

Headings feature tight tracking and deliberate optical weights to convey scale and confidence. Body text maintains open apertures and standard tracking to optimize readability across dispatch tables, repair logs, and passenger schedules on high-density displays.

## Layout & Spacing

The system runs on an 8pt spatial grid deployed through a fluid 12-column bento layout on desktop, transitioning to 6 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile. 

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column grid, `margin-lg` (4rem) outer margins, and `gutter-lg` (2rem) gutters. Information-rich panels span asymmetric module spans (e.g., 8-column fleet tracker + 4-column workshop telemetry).
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 6-column grid with `margin-md` (2.5rem) outer padding, collapsing wide horizontal bentos into 3x3 paired modules.
- **Mobile (under 768px):** 4-column single-stream stack using `margin` (1.5rem) and `gutter-sm` (1rem). Touch targets maintain minimum 48px heights with clear visual separators between service sections.

## Elevation & Depth

Spatial depth relies on clean tonal stratification and ultra-diffused, ambient drop shadows rather than heavy borders or layered skeletal structures.

- **Base Canvas:** Tinted `#FAFAFC` warm base creating soft contrast with pure components.
- **Card Tier 1 (Resting Bento):** `#FFFFFF` background, paired with an ambient tinted shadow (`0 10px 30px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`), framed by a low-contrast hairline stroke (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`).
- **Card Tier 2 (Interactive / Hover):** Elevated shadow (`0 20px 40px -10px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.09)`) accompanied by a subtle -2px vertical translation.
- **Floating Modals & Dispatch Overlays:** Deep, diffused atmospheric depth (`0 25px 50px -12px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.16)`) over a backdrop blur of `8px` at 40% slate opacity.

## Shapes

The geometric architecture uses softened, sweeping contours to evoke hospitality, approachable service, and modern aerodynamics:

- **Bento Modules & Master Containers:** Receive `rounded-xl` (1.5rem / 24px) for substantial structural containment.
- **Buttons, Form Inputs & Media Wrappers:** Use standard `rounded` (0.5rem / 8px) to `rounded-lg` (1rem / 16px) for tactile ergonomics.
- **Badges, Filter Pills & Status Indicators:** Use fully rounded capsules (`rounded-full` / 9999px) to contrast against rectilinear card bounds.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Transport:** High-saturation `#F59E0B` fill with `#0F172A` bold text, rounded to 16px, padding `0.875rem 1.75rem`. Elevates with a warm amber glow shadow on hover.
- **Primary Workshop:** Solid `#2563EB` fill with `#FFFFFF` text, rounded to 16px. Designed for diagnostic and booking actions.
- **Direct WhatsApp:** Rich emerald `#10B981` fill with `#FFFFFF` text and official icon prefix.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Ultra-light tinted fill (`#F1F5F9`) with `#0F172A` text, expanding on interaction with an inner ring.

### Bento Cards & Service Tiles
Bento cards serve as the primary content containers. Each card features an optional subtle colored accent line (2px top border or glowing badge) indicating its domain: Amber for passenger transit or Cobalt for vehicle repair. Internal padding follows `space-lg` (1.5rem) or `space-xl` (2.5rem).

### Status Badges & Pills
Constructed as pill shapes with semi-transparent background tints (12% opacity of the base hue) paired with saturated text:
- *Active Route / On-Duty:* Tinted amber background with `#D97706` text.
- *Certified / Service Completed:* Tinted emerald background with `#059669` text.
- *In Repair / Diagnostics:* Tinted cobalt background with `#1D4ED8` text.

### Inputs & Selectors
Text inputs utilize a `#FFFFFF` fill nested within an off-white section, bounded by a 1.5px border of `#E2E8F0` with a 12px corner radius. Focused inputs transition the border to `#0F172A` or service-specific blue, accompanied by a soft 3px diffuse focus ring.

### Lists & Tables
Repair logs, fleet sheets, and route tables feature alternate row fills using `#F8FAFC`, with generous vertical cell padding (16px) to maximize legibility for logistical operations.
